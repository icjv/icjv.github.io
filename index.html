01 / About the conference

Better decisions.
Stronger evidence.

ICJV brings together researchers studying JeV and related models that turn context into structured decisions for software.

We welcome work on model design, calibrated probabilities, efficient inference, and real-world decision systems. Our focus is on understanding when these models work, when they fail, and how to evaluate them fairly.

Original methods, careful replications, benchmark contributions, and well-supported negative results all belong in the conversation.

ICJV is an independently organized conference. No affiliation with or endorsement by TypeSafe AI or ICLR is implied.
02 / Call for papers

From model to decision.

We invite research on JeV and the broader System One ecosystem, including the following themes.

01 — FOUNDATIONS

Decision model design

Architectures, training objectives, distillation, constrained outputs, and alternatives to autoregressive decision making.

02 — UNCERTAINTY

Calibration & abstention

Probability quality, uncertainty estimates, distribution shift, selective prediction, and knowing when to defer.

03 — SYSTEMS

Efficient inference

Latency, throughput, serving cost, edge deployment, and reproducible comparisons under realistic workloads.

04 — APPLICATIONS

Routing & agent control

Semantic routing, tool selection, workflow decisions, and coordination between fast decision models and reasoning models.

05 — RELIABILITY

Safety & robustness

Adversarial evaluation, failure analysis, human oversight, and reliable decision boundaries in deployed systems.

06 — EVIDENCE

Benchmarks & replication

Datasets, evaluation protocols, open implementations, reproducibility studies, and informative negative results.

04 / Open scientific exchange

Public review.
Independent judgment.

Our proposed review process follows an ICLR-inspired approach: a public scientific record with double-anonymous reviewing.

01 / SUBMIT

Share the evidence

Anonymized papers and revisions become publicly readable after the submission deadline.

02 / REVIEW

Independent assessment

We plan three human reviews per paper, with expertise matching and conflict-of-interest checks.

03 / DISCUSS

Respond and refine

Anonymous reviews, author responses, and moderated public discussion support a transparent exchange.

04 / DECIDE

Keep the record open

Meta-reviews and decisions are public. Both accepted and rejected papers retain their public review records.

Openness with clear boundaries. Reviewer identities remain anonymous; author identities are protected during double-anonymous review. Private contact details, conflicts, and administrative communications are not part of the public record. AI assistance must be disclosed and protect confidentiality; human chairs make final decisions. These settings are requested and will be finalized after venue deployment.
05 / For authors

Make your claims
testable.

Strong submissions explain their assumptions, document their methods, and show the limits of their conclusions.

  • Report decision quality and probability calibration.
  • Measure latency and cost under stated conditions.
  • Include appropriate baselines and failure cases.
  • Document data, model versions, and evaluation settings.
  • Provide reproducibility materials where possible.

What kinds of contributions are welcome?

Original research, replications, benchmark and dataset studies, and well-supported negative results relevant to JeV and System One decision models.

Do I need to use the commercial JeV model?

No. Work on related System One decision models and open implementations is within scope, provided its connection to the conference themes is clear.
